Accepting a friend.

Accepting used to do nothing you could see — the page refreshed and the row was somewhere else. These are the four studies the built version was chosen from; it blends D and A. Tap Accept in any screen.

What was chosen — 2026-09-08

D’s confirmation joined to A’s travel, with the undo dropped. Tapping Accept turns the row green where you tapped it and it reads Added Ming; after a beat it lifts, carries down into Your people, and the green drains out of it as it settles into an ordinary row. No undo: nobody accepts a friend by accident, and offering to take it back implies the other person has already been told something we’d have to un-tell them.

One thing the built version makes obvious that these studies don’t: when Waiting on you sits directly above Your people, the row itself only travels about eight pixels. The distance is real but it is mostly closed rather than crossed — the section above collapses and the list below rises to meet a row that barely moves. It reads well, but the movement belongs to the page more than to the row.

Live in apps/web/app/friends/friends-client.tsx and the “Accepting someone” block of globals.css. Reduced-motion keeps the confirmation and drops the travel. This file is the shareable copy — open it in any browser, no build step.
