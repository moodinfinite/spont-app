Why this one works as the first thing you see

An empty feed is the state people hit most, and the risk is that it reads as a broken app rather than a quiet week. This is the only option that shows the engine doing its job — you can watch it check.

Decisions baked into it

  • Grey is busy, green is a real opening. Only gaps long enough to hold a hangout light up, so green always means “usable”, never just “free”.
  • The scan is sequential, one day at a time. Sweeping all seven at once looks decorative; one at a time looks like searching.
  • Found windows stay lit rather than fading with the scan. The end state is a readable week, not an animation you had to catch.
  • It loops slowly — about nine seconds per pass, then a pause. Fast enough to notice, slow enough not to nag.
  • Frozen under reduced motion, landing straight on the found state.

What it can’t do

It needs your calendar connected and it needs at least a few busy blocks to look like anything — a completely empty week renders as one long green bar, which is honest but says little. Worth pairing with the plainer copy for that case.
