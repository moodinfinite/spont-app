Six abstract marks for the sync moment.

Each loops a full cycle — searching, then finding, then resetting — so you can see the resolve, which is the part that matters. All six say the same thing in different accents: two schedules becoming one open slot.

OverlapLiteral

Your existing mark, given a search-and-settle cycle. Safest choice — it's already the logo, so the loading screen teaches the brand.

Grid scanOn-theme

A week filling up with busy, until one square refuses to fill and flares open. The most honest picture of what Spont actually does.

Slot alignStrongest idea

Two calendars scrolling past each other until their gaps line up and a free window locks in. Reads as a machine searching — which is literally what's happening.

Phase lockMost abstract

Two rhythms drifting out of step, then falling into sync. Prettiest and least literal — risks reading as audio rather than calendars.

RippleAmbient

Two people sending out signals; where the waves meet, the plan appears. Calm and ambient — good if the screen holds longer than a second.

WeaveWarmest

Two paths drawing themselves until they cross and knot. The only one that feels like people rather than data — least techy of the six.

All six are built to loop, but the real screen resolves once. Whichever you pick, the found-state is the frame that matters — it's what someone sees at the moment the connect actually finishes, and it should hand off cleanly into the “Calendar connected” beat rather than snapping back to the start.
